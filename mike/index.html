for our future selves

If we ever read this years from now, I hope we are somewhere quiet and familiar, somewhere that already carries the shape of our life together. I hope I still look at you with the same disbelief I feel now, like somehow I was trusted with someone this gentle, this funny, this alive, and this impossible to replace.

I picture a future where loving you is built into the structure of my days. I wake up next to you and reach for you before I am fully awake. I know the sound of your footsteps in the next room. I know which version of your silence means you are tired, which one means you are thinking, and which one means you only need me to sit beside you for a while. I picture dinners that are nothing special until they are, your head on my shoulder, your laugh interrupting whatever stress the day tried to leave behind, and a home that feels warm because you are in it.

I want to keep becoming the kind of person who makes your life softer. I want to be someone you can lean on without asking, someone who still chooses you in the boring seasons, the hard seasons, the ordinary Tuesdays, and every future birthday after this one. I want a life where we keep collecting private languages, dumb jokes, small rituals, and reasons to stay close. If the future version of me is reading this beside you, then I hope he never forgot that this was the dream: not perfection, not spectacle, just the miracle of getting to love you for a very long time.

Always reaching for you,
Mark Mu
